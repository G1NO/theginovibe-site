    Hey, I'm Gino.

    I'm not sure if I'm from this planet or not. I have consciousness here on Earth, though there are many times where I feel out of place... which is why I play music.

    I find a connection to nature and music brings me back to peace, though at times, the right humans spark the most magical connections... which is why I think we're here.

    I lived in Belize for 10 years playing music barefoot and shirtless on an island. It was the simple life... until it wasn't... everything changes.

    Now I'm here in Southern California trying to play music more often, though bills seem to get in the way a lot more. So if you'd like to hear me play more music, please support me on Patreon or feel free to hire me for your next event.
